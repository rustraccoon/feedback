# RustRaccoon feedback

Found a problem on [www.rustraccoon.com](https://www.rustraccoon.com)? Tell us here.

[**Report a problem**](https://github.com/rustraccoon/feedback/issues/new/choose)

A broken link, a typo, a page that looks wrong on your phone, something slow or confusing: all of it helps.

## Before you post

- Everything written here is public. Do not include passwords, phone numbers or details about your business that you would not want others to read.
- For anything private, or to talk about a project, email [info@rustraccoon.com](mailto:info@rustraccoon.com).

This repository holds reports only. It does not contain the website's code.
